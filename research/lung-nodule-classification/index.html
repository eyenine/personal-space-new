Bachelor's Thesis: Lung Nodule Classification

A transfer-learning ensemble for classifying lung nodules from CT images, with explainable AI layered on top, later extended into a published paper.

Biomedical AIExplainable AIPrior Chapter
Problem
Lung nodule classification models are often accurate but opaque, limiting clinical trust in their output.
Motivation
An early interest in making AI decisions inspectable rather than just performant, the same instinct now carried into security research.
Methods
Transfer learning with an ensemble of pretrained CNN backbones, combined with explainable AI (XAI) techniques to surface which image regions drove each classification.
Datasets
LIDC-IDRI (Lung Image Database Consortium and Image Database Resource Initiative) CT dataset.

publication

LungCT-NET: An Explainable Transfer Learning-Based Robust Ensemble Model for Lung Cancer Diagnosis

An explainable, transfer-learning-based ensemble model for lung cancer diagnosis, published in Knowledge-Based Systems (Q1, Elsevier).
