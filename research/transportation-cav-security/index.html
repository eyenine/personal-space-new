Transportation & CAV Security

Securing the connected and automated vehicles, V2X links, and intelligent transportation infrastructure that Houston's roads increasingly run on.

Transportation CybersecurityCAV/V2X SecurityIntelligent Transportation Systems
Problem
Connected and automated vehicles extend a city's attack surface onto public roads: a compromised V2X message or ITS controller isn't just a data breach, it's a physical safety risk.
Motivation
This is the core of what drew me to UH and CYBER-CARE: cybersecurity research with an immediate, physical-world stake, in a city where transportation infrastructure is actively being modernized.
Current work
Just beginning: reviewing the CAV/V2X security literature and identifying an initial thesis-scale problem within CYBER-CARE's transportation security agenda.
Future directions
Threat modeling for V2X communication; anomaly detection for connected-vehicle networks; resilience of ITS control systems under attack.
